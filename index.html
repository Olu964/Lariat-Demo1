Our vision

LOur
visionLARIAT · TEXAS

Texas moves fast when the Legislature is in session, and most businesses find out what changed only after it already has. Lariat exists to close that gap.

I started this because understanding what’s happening at the Texas State Capitol shouldn’t require a law degree or a lobbyist on retainer. A bill that reshapes permitting timelines or reporting requirements matters just as much to a small business owner as it does to a trade association with a government affairs team, but right now only one of those groups has the tools to see it coming.

I’m building Lariat to change that: plain English summaries of real bills, organized by the industries they actually touch, so a business can see what’s relevant to them without wading through legislative text written for lawyers.

Right now that means a free demo built on real, historical session data. Over time it means live tracking through every session, real automated alerts the moment something relevant is filed, and enough runway on the data side that I can go beyond bills to city ordinances and regulatory changes too.

The goal isn’t just to summarize what happens at the Texas State Capitol. It’s to give every business in Texas, not just the ones who can afford a lobbyist, a fair shot at seeing what’s coming before it’s already law.

Free-use demo notice

This is a free-use demo to be used up until a select time of which it will be released behind an email.

Some bills in this demo are summarized from actual official bill text and are generally 50–200 words long. Other bills currently use available metadata because a viable source document was not found; these summaries are intentionally shorter at 30–40 words. Source coverage and automation are still being improved, and this issue is expected to be addressed before the full website release.
