Privacy Policy

Lariat is a prototype that turns Texas bill summaries into clear, business-ready signals. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

✦ Effective August 19, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Lariat demo site — including the landing page, the Bill feed, Pricing, and the optional email subscription flow. It describes the current prototype, which may be served locally or deployed with a separately configured backend. Browsing the feed does not require an account, payment, or login. Billing, customer accounts, and automatic bill-alert delivery are not active features in the current code.

2. Information we collect

The current application collects or processes the following information:

  • Email address — entered when you request a subscription verification email.
  • Industry preference — the industry selected for a subscription.
  • Verification code and private access code — the verification code is checked by the backend and stored only as a salted scrypt hash while pending; it expires after 10 minutes and is never stored in plaintext. The private access code is checked and is not stored.
  • Browser storage — bill notes, light/dark theme preference, selected demo plan, subscription badge state, and the signed unsubscribe token associated with a verified subscription are stored in localStorage on the device.
  • Transient network information — the backend reads the connecting IP address to apply rate limits and enforce a three-failure access-code lockout. The backend stores an HMAC-derived key and lockout timestamps, not the raw IP address, in the local subscription data file. A hosting provider, reverse proxy, firewall, or server process may keep normal access logs.

We do not ask for your name, payment information, or precise location. Do not put confidential information into bill notes: localStorage is user-controlled and is not an encrypted workspace.

3. How we use your information

  • Your email address and industry are used to send the verification and welcome emails. The current backend does not contain a scheduled bill-alert delivery job; if alerts are enabled later, they will be sent only for the industries you select.
  • The verification code confirms control of the email address; it is used for no other purpose and is not stored in plaintext.
  • The IP address is used transiently for abuse prevention and rate limiting.
  • Bill notes, theme preference, selected demo plan, subscription badge state, and the unsubscribe token remain in browser storage. The selected demo plan controls the number of industry subscriptions the demo UI permits; it is not payment or account authorization.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Where your information lives

In your browser. The feed stores notes, theme preference, selected demo plan, subscription badge state, and the signed unsubscribe token in localStorage. These values remain on the device until the user clears them or the site removes them.

On the backend host. In the local prototype, the backend stores a subscription record containing the email address, industry, verification time, and signed unsubscribe token in server/data/subscriptions.json, outside the public site directory. Pending verification codes are stored only as salted scrypt hashes with a 10-minute expiry. Failed access-code lockout records contain an HMAC-derived network key, a failure count, and timestamps; raw IP addresses are not written to the file. A production deployment must replace this local JSON file with the encrypted database and access controls described in the project's security checklist.

With our email provider. To deliver verification and welcome emails, the backend sends the recipient email address, sender information, message content, and selected industry to Brevo. Brevo's own privacy policy governs its processing. No payment information is collected by the current subscription flow.

With hosting and infrastructure providers. A public deployment may expose request metadata, such as IP address, timestamps, user agent, and requested URL, to its hosting provider or reverse proxy according to that provider's logging policy.

5. Third-party services

  • Brevo — delivers verification and welcome emails when you subscribe. See their privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — loads the site's typography. Google may log standard request information when the fonts load; see Google's privacy policy.
  • Texas Legislature — the feed links out to capitol.texas.gov so you can verify bill status with the official source. That site is outside our control.

6. Cookies and tracking

The current frontend does not set application cookies and does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting code. It uses localStorage for notes, theme, selected demo plan, subscription badge state, and the signed unsubscribe token. The Free, Professional, and Business choices are demo-only browser state; billing, payment processing, customer accounts, and server-side plan authorization are not active. When the subscription flow is used, the browser sends the entered subscription fields to the configured Lariat backend; hosting infrastructure and third-party services may maintain their own technical logs.

7. How long we keep information

  • Verification codes expire after 10 minutes, are stored only as salted scrypt hashes, and are removed when verified or when expired records are pruned during a later subscription request or verification.
  • Access-code lockout records are removed after the 24-hour lockout window or after the associated failure window expires. They contain an HMAC-derived network key rather than the raw IP address.
  • Subscriptions remain in the configured backend store until unsubscribed or deleted by the operator. The local prototype does not implement an automatic retention period. The signed unsubscribe token expires after the configured token lifetime, 90 days by default.
  • Browser data — notes, theme preference, selected demo plan, subscription badge state, and the unsubscribe token remain until removed by the site, changed or cleared through the relevant demo controls, or cleared in browser settings. The reset-subscriptions control does not clear the selected plan.
  • Infrastructure logs — retention is controlled by the hosting provider, reverse proxy, and operator's logging settings. The intended operational target is 365 days where the provider supports it, but the current local prototype does not implement automatic log retention or deletion.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Stop receiving alerts: click Unsubscribe on the Bill feed, or open the unsubscribe link in any Lariat alert email and confirm the action. This removes your record from the local backend.
  • Clear browser state: use Reset demo subscriptions on the Bill feed, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Reset clears the browser subscription badge and local subscription state, but it does not clear the selected demo plan, delete the backend subscription, or bypass a server-enforced access-code lockout.
  • Access or correct: in the local prototype, the operator can inspect the backend data file and users can inspect their browser storage. For a public deployment, contact the operator at the address below to request access, correction, or deletion.

Unsubscribe requests require the signed token issued after email verification. For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the details in section 11.

9. Children's privacy

Lariat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has shared information with us, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Security

The backend uses salted scrypt hashes for pending verification codes, short expirations, limited verification attempts, three failed access-code attempts followed by a 24-hour lockout, request limits, strict input handling, signed expiring unsubscribe tokens, and server-side email delivery. Brevo requests use HTTPS. The development server binds to 127.0.0.1 by default and keeps subscription data outside the public site directory. A public deployment must use HTTPS, a maintained reverse proxy, secret management, a production database, backups, monitoring, and the full security checklist; the current prototype does not provide those infrastructure controls by itself.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your information, contact us at lariatfeed@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest version. Please check back here for changes. This policy describes the current prototype and is not legal advice; obtain a jurisdiction-specific privacy review before a public launch.

Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Our commitment

Lariat is designed to support keyboard and assistive-technology users, but this prototype has not been formally audited or certified against WCAG. We use WCAG 2.1 Level AA as a development target and welcome reports of barriers.

What we've built in

  • Native links, buttons, headings, lists, labels, forms, and dialog elements are used throughout the site.
  • Visible :focus-visible styles are provided for interactive controls, and the primary interactions are keyboard-operable.
  • Form fields, navigation landmarks, dialog labels, status messages, and decorative elements have accessibility markup where implemented.
  • Support for prefers-reduced-motion disables or minimizes the decorative animation and smooth scrolling.
  • Bill cards, subscription feedback, plan-limit messages, the access-code lockout countdown, and note-save feedback expose labels or live status text to assistive technologies where implemented.
  • The Pricing page uses three keyboard-operable plan-selection links, and the Bill Feed displays the selected demo plan and current industry usage.

Conformance status

Not formally evaluated. No independent WCAG audit, screen-reader test, keyboard-only test, or color-contrast certification has been completed. Conformance is therefore not claimed, and some success criteria may not be met.

Known limitations

  • The site must be served over HTTP by the local backend or another web server; opening the HTML files with file:// is unsupported.
  • The subscription flow requires a reachable, correctly configured backend. In local development that is node server/server.js; a public deployment needs a separate production configuration.
  • Some visual content is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. The dynamic canvas, custom visual styling, native dialog behavior, dynamically rendered bill list, plan-limit messages, and live lockout countdown need browser and assistive-technology testing.
  • Plan selection is demo-only browser state stored in localStorage; it is not an account, payment control, or server-side authorization feature.
  • Focus restoration and behavior can vary by browser, especially for native dialogs and older browsers.
  • Color contrast has not been independently measured in every light/dark state, and third-party content or websites are outside Lariat's control.

Feedback

For an accessibility barrier, please contact lariatfeed@gmail.com with the page, task, assistive technology or browser involved, and the problem you encountered. We will review reports and work toward an accessible fix.
